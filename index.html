Read Me

First and foremost, please don't assume this simulator is 100% correct. While I've tried my best to mimic the in-game battles, I don't have access to the exact equations the developers use. Treat this tool as a helpful "second opinion" rather than a "true predictor".

Huge thanks to LexAeterna for providing the data that allowed me to extrapolate the attack speed breakpoints. It is hard to overstate how tedious collecting that data is. Analyzing and extrapolating it is the easy part. Once again, thanks a lot, LexAeterna.

Attack Speed Breakpoints

For those unaware, an Attack Speed Breakpoint is the specific threshold required to jump to the next attack speed bracket. For instance, with a Quantum Gun or Infernal Trident between 100% and 149.9% Attack Speed, your double attack interval will be 1.0s. But the moment you hit 150.0%, that interval drops to 0.8s. While some weapons share the same breakpoints, most differ. Furthermore, even if two weapons have the same breakpoints, varying projectile speeds will lead to slightly different hit timings. Note that for single attacks, every weapon shares the same breakpoint regardless of whether it's melee or ranged. For example, if you have 132% attack speed, no matter what weapon or skin you wear, the single attack interval is always 0.8s.

To be safe, you may want to aim for 0.5% extra attack speed above the values listed in the table for your actual build. Because of potential rounding quirks, I cannot 100% guarantee the exact precision of these breakpoints.

Weapon Skins

Weapon skins affect the first hit timing and double attack breakpoints. This means you can strategically decide whether to equip a skin to gain an advantage. For example, if you equip a Quantum Gun with 138% Attack Speed, it is better to use a skin like the Druid Sword. This way, instead of doing double attacks every 1.0s with the gun, you will do double attacks every 0.9s with the Druid Sword.

On the other hand, if you have 152% attack speed with a Quantum Gun, it's better to just use the Quantum Gun instead of the Druid Sword so that the double attack interval is 0.8s instead of 0.9s.

Please note that for the melee version of some skins, I had to make a lot of assumptions to get the breakpoints. Collecting data for melee is much harder because there aren't that many melee users. If a skin is not listed, it means I don't have enough data to extrapolate the breakpoints yet.

Early Game

According to LexAeterna, after the Ascension patch, the last-second nuke is only viable for at most 1 week. By the time he got the new tier skills, the damage was not significant enough to do the last-second nuke with a regen build. Everyone's HP and damage from their equipment is strong enough to negate most of the skill damage.

He then came up with a new idea: use a regen build, make sure you have 0% double chance, and figure out the attack speed so that your last hit always lands as close to the 60-second mark as possible. This strategy is not perfect because the opponent may hit after your last attack, or you might not fully regen from the opponent's last hit. However, he said that this strategy is reliable enough for the arena.

Known Flaws With My Tool

1. Ranged vs Melee: For ranged weapons with projectiles, my simulator's subsequent hit timings might be off by a fraction of a second. In general, it shouldn't be a big deal, but please be aware that I simplified the tool to have the same interval regardless of the distance from the opponent.

2. DPS: While my tool displays DPS values, be aware that these numbers are often misleading. For instance, if the battle ends in 5 seconds, the drone won't have hit the opponent yet. However, the DPS calculation still takes the drone damage into account. Another example is if you reduce your crit chance for more crit damage. The displayed DPS value may be higher, but your battle will depend on RNG even more. Practically speaking, any attack that is not a crit is negligible to the overall damage. In the endgame, crit chance behaves more like a hit accuracy stat rather than actual crit chance.

Final Thoughts

I have had some feedback saying that this simulator is decent. At the end of the day, this is just a random tool created by a random person. Please don't take the simulator's results as absolute fact. It is far from perfect, and at worst, it could be misleading.
